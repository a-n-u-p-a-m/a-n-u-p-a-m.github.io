Reverse-Engineering a Temporal Graph Network

Mechanistic Interpretability · Temporal Modeling · Causal Evaluation

Jun – Oct 2026

Trained a 121,546-parameter Temporal Graph Network to detect A→B→C temporal motifs within a Δt window, then took it apart. A hand-rolled GRU exposes 19 intervention points for activation patching, ablation, gate clamping and candidate-state replacement across 17 experiments. Established that the model implements independent accumulation rather than compositional routing, and that the GRU gates' motif-specific deviation is causally inert despite being almost perfectly discriminable observationally. Treated instrument validity as a result in its own right — ceiling arms verify every patching claim, one permutation control was rejected as an invalid null, and a sign reversal in my own earlier analysis is documented rather than dropped.

Mechanistic Interpretability of Cross-Language Brain Alignment

Interpretability · Computational Neuroscience · NLP

Mar – May 2026

Benchmarked five transformer architecture families against the fMRI of 84 subjects (49 English, 35 Chinese) listening to The Little Prince, across 200 cortical regions. Led the sparse-autoencoder and normalization-layer work: five SAE variants built from scratch over Qwen-2.5-0.5B hidden states, evaluated by ridge regression with hemodynamic lag stacking. Found that SAE features at Layer 11 peak at R = 0.079 with zero-shot English→Chinese transfer at R = 0.064, outperforming every normalization baseline by ~75% — and that the SAE cannot reconstruct across languages while still predicting across them, which is what shows the features are semantic rather than language-shaped.

Algorithmic Collusion Detection via Incentive-Compatible Audits

Reinforcement Learning · Game Theory · Mechanism Design

Mar – Apr 2026

Replicated Calvano et al. (2020) to confirm that independent Q-Learning and SARSA pricing agents converge to supracompetitive joint-monopoly pricing with no communication, then reframed detection as a mechanism design problem rather than a pattern-recognition one. Designed an audit protocol ℳ = (T, τ, F) that injects a synthetic competitive entrant, making honest price revelation weakly dominant. Derived the minimum audit duration T* beyond which mimicking competition costs more than the expected fine, proved its comparative statics — including the counterintuitive result that raising the fine makes detection harder — and verified both numerically across seven seeds.

Spectral Soil Modeler

Machine Learning · Applied Spectroscopy · Full-Stack ML

Oct – Dec 2025

End-to-end ML application predicting soil properties from hyperspectral data, with 15 model–technique combinations across five algorithms and three spectral transforms. Built a dual-paradigm architecture running fixed-hyperparameter and CV-tuned pipelines in parallel, so the contribution of tuning is measurable rather than assumed, and implemented a custom Cubist estimator — gradient-boosted rules blended with k-NN smoothing — because the field-standard model has no mature Python implementation. Wired an LLM analytics layer over all 15 results, confined to explaining numbers computed in Python rather than computing any itself.

Detection of Computer-Generated Text

NLP · Deep Learning · Machine Learning

Mar – May 2023

Raced eleven classifiers — classical ML on TF-IDF and on BERT embeddings, character-level CharCNN and GRU models, and fine-tuned BERT and RoBERTa — on the same 25,572 TweepFake tweets, varying representation independently of architecture. Transformers reached 89.5%, but the finding that aged best came from the failure analysis: detection degrades with text length and breaks down below roughly 85–189 words, which is the structural reason every AI-text detector since carries the same caveat.

Implicit Hate-Speech Detection and Counter-Speech Generation

NLP · Deep Learning · Generation

Oct – Dec 2023

Targeted hate speech that never uses a slur — where the harm is carried by presupposition rather than vocabulary, and lexical filters see nothing. Paired pre-trained toxicity classifiers with an emotion classifier, on the argument that counter-speech has to match register to land, then built two routes to generation: prompt-based with GPT-2 and GPT-Neo, and GPT-2 fine-tuned on the expert-written Multi-Target CONAN corpus. Published a 15-epoch trace of the model learning to argue, which shows format being acquired before content and learning that is anything but monotone.
