About

Software Systems Development (SSD) is a graduate-level course that provides an in-depth understanding of principles, practices, and tools used in building robust and scalable software systems. Topics span Operating Systems, Networks, Databases, and Software Engineering. Students gain practical exposure through labs and projects using technologies such as Bash, SQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Flask, and Python.

Course Policy

The SSD course promotes practical system building with academic honesty and real-world collaboration. Use of AI tools like ChatGPT is allowed with clear mention in submissions. Deadlines are strict, and plagiarism will lead to penalties.

  • AI Tools: Allowed responsibly; mention usage in reports.
  • Attendance: Mandatory for lectures and labs.
  • Grading: Assignments 15%, Labs 15%, Activities 10%, Quizzes 10%, Mid 10%, Final 40%.
